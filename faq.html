Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about orders, delivery, returns, and support.

How long does delivery take inside Tripoli and other cities?

• Inside Tripoli: Same-day delivery for all orders. (Same Day)

• Outside Tripoli (other Libyan cities): 2–3 business days. (2-3 Days)

What is the return & exchange period?

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 3 days of delivery. Items must be unused with all tags and original packaging.

(3 days)
